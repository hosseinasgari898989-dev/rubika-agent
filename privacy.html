Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Rubika Agent is designed to minimize the data used for its social-video automation features.

Information handled

When you authorize a supported integration, the service may receive profile information, account statistics, public video metadata, and authorization tokens needed for requested features.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate the connected account, display authorized account information, prepare publishing workflows, and make publishing requests you initiate or explicitly authorize.

Security

Client secrets and long-lived authorization credentials are intended to be kept on the secure server-side integration layer rather than in the public website code.

Sharing

Rubika Agent does not intentionally sell personal information. Data may be transmitted to the connected platform when necessary to provide a feature requested by the user.

Retention and removal

Connected-account data should be retained only as long as needed for the integration. Users may disconnect the integration through the applicable platform controls.

Third-party policies

Use of TikTok remains subject to TikTok's own privacy policy and terms.
